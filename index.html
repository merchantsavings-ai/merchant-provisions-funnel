Common questions

Is the review really free?

Yes. The effective rate breakdown and the initial review across processing, financing, and insurance and benefits are free, and you get the breakdown in writing with no obligation to switch or buy anything.

What do you need for the processing breakdown?

A recent processing statement is best, because it shows your real volume, fees, and card mix. If you do not have one handy, start with the calculator and the form, and David will tell you exactly what to send.

Do I have to switch providers?

No. The point of putting it in writing is so you can compare it fairly with what you have now. You decide whether a change makes sense.

What does free equipment mean?

Free equipment is available for qualifying businesses. Whether your business qualifies depends on your setup and volume, and David will confirm that in your review before you commit to anything.

Can financing really cover no credit check?

The lender platforms we work with cover a wide range, from prime through no credit check options. Approval and terms depend on the lender and the customer, and not every applicant will qualify for every option.

Which service should I start with?

Most owners start with processing because the statement makes the cost easy to see. If you are losing sales at checkout, start with financing. If coverage or benefits are up for renewal, start there. You can check more than one box on the form.
